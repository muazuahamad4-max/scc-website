Governance Philosophy

SCC governance is built on accountability, clarity of roles, and documented processes. Leadership is responsibility-driven, not personality-driven.

Scope of SCC

SCC supports initiatives in digital commerce structures, ethical participation, skills empowerment, and regional collaboration. SCC does not operate commercial ventures directly unless governance conditions are met.

Transparency & Review

All frameworks, drafts, and standards developed under SCC are subject to periodic review and internal revision. Opacity is treated as institutional risk.

Status

This governance page is a living document. Updates are expected, but stability is prioritized over frequent change.
