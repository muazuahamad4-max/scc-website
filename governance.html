Governance Structure

The Sidra Center of Commerce (SCC) operates under a structured governance model ensuring transparency, accountability, and ethical business practices across all its operations.

  • Board of Directors: Oversees strategic direction and compliance.
  • Operations Team: Manages day-to-day activities and projects.
  • Advisory Council: Provides expertise, mentorship, and policy guidance.

Core Policies

  • Ethical business practices and transparency in all operations.
  • Inclusive participation for individuals and communities.
  • Continuous learning and capacity building initiatives.
