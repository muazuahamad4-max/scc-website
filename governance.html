Governance Structure

The Sidra Center of Commerce (SCC) operates with a transparent governance framework designed to ensure accountability and ethical decision-making.

Our governance includes a board of advisors, operational committees, and clear policy guidelines for all stakeholders.

Core Policies

  • Ethical Business Practices
  • Transparent Financial Reporting
  • Stakeholder Engagement and Feedback
  • Compliance with Regional Regulations
