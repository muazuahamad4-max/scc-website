Who We Are

Sidra Center of Commerce (SCC) bridges digital utility with real-world commerce through structured governance and ethical growth. We empower individuals, communities, and businesses to thrive in a trusted and transparent environment.

Our Mission

To provide education, skills, and business models that combine technology with ethical commerce, enabling sustainable growth for our stakeholders.

Our Vision

To become a globally trusted center that empowers regional collaborations and digital innovation in commerce.
