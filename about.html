About SCC

The Sidra Center of Commerce (SCC) bridges digital utility with real-world commerce through structured governance, ethical business practices, and skill empowerment.

We provide resources, mentorship, and collaboration opportunities for individuals and businesses across regions.

Mission & Vision

Mission: Serve as a bridge between digital utility and real-world commerce through education, skills, and ethical business models.

Vision: Trusted global center empowering individuals, communities, and regional collaborations.
